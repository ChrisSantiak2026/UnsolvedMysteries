The Mary Celeste

Atlantic Ocean — December 1872

The Event

The brigantine Mary Celeste was found adrift in the Atlantic, heading toward the Strait of Gibraltar. The ship was in seaworthy condition, under partial sail, with a six-month supply of food and water—yet not a single soul remained on board. The lifeboat was missing, but the crew’s personal belongings were undisturbed.

Evidence Locker

Current Theories

  • Alcoholic vapor explosion in the cargo hold
  • Waterspout causing localized panic and evacuation
  • Piracy (rejected due to intact personal valuables)

The Paper Trail
