The Dead Internet

Global Web — Emergent 2020s

The Event

The 'Dead Internet Theory' posits that the once-organic, human-driven web has been overtaken by AI-generated content and bot activity. Proponents argue that the vast majority of online interaction—social media posts, comments, and even news articles—is now a synthetic feedback loop designed to manipulate human behavior and consumerism.

Evidence Locker

Current Theories

The Archive questions the boundaries of modern digital reality.

  • Algorithmic Capture Engagement-based algorithms naturally favor high-volume bot content, leading to a 'dead' environment where human voices are drowned out by automated efficiency.
  • Mass Psychological Operation Speculation suggests that state actors and corporations use synthetic traffic to engineer public opinion and social cohesion through manufactured consensus.
