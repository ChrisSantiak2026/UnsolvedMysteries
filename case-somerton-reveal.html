The Final Identity

Adelaide, Australia — Solved July 2022

The Event

After 74 years of silence, the man found on Somerton Beach in 1948 was identified through modern genetic genealogy. Working with degraded DNA from hair found in the original death mask, researchers identified him as Carl 'Charles' Webb, a Melbourne-born electrical engineer. While the *who* is solved, the *why*—his coded book and lack of identification—remains an enigma.

Evidence Locker

Current Theories

The Archive closes the identity but reopens the narrative.

  • The Lone Voyager New evidence suggests Webb was a man who had recently separated from his wife and was traveling alone across state lines, potentially suffering from personal depression.
  • The Lingering Code Despite identifying the man, the purpose of the 'Tamám Shud' scrap and the cryptic code written in his copy of the *Rubaiyat* has never been cracked.
